PROBLEM 5
SSE reductions as K increases: 1→2: 550; 2→3: 230; 3→4: 70; 4→5: 20; 5→6: 10.
A reasonable elbow choice is K=3: the large improvements through K=3 taper substantially afterward. K=6 has the lowest SSE largely because adding clusters cannot increase the best achievable SSE; it may over-segment the data and is not automatically the most meaningful solution. The sharp decline from K=1 to K=3 indicates that allowing a few clusters greatly improves compactness, but it does not alone prove the data contains exactly three natural groups.
